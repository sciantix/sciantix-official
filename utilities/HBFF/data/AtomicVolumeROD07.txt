# Data description
# rod 07 disc 08 UO2, 103.5 GWd/tHM (NFIR program): a standard-grain UO2 disc (3D grain size 18 µm, NFD fabrication) cut from rod 07 of the HBWR IFA-649 irradiation, sandwiched between Mo discs in the "hot" position of the rod, reaching 103.5 GWd/tHM with calculated local temperatures of 560–700 °C, and the HBS formed over the entire disc. Its gas volume is not directly measured but deduced from the EPMA/SIMS Xe data plus the SEM porosity of about 11%, assuming the bubble gas Xe/Kr ratio equals the punctured-gas ratio of 8.8, giving about 9 × 10⁻⁵ m³/mol, i.e. roughly 149 Å³/at (about 3.6 times the UO2 trivacancy volume)
