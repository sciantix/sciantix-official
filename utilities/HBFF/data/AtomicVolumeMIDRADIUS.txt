# Data description
# MOX mid-radius from [5]. MIMAS MOX fuel from commercial LWR pellets, measured at mid-radius position, from the earlier CEA study [5]. This series extends to much higher local burn-up than the disc, because the plutonium-rich agglomerates in MOX reach very high local burn-up in the pellet interior while the fuel there stays at high temperature without full HBS restructuring.
MIMAS MOX fuel sampled both in the mid-radius agglomerates (local burn-ups from about 80 up to 180 GWd/t). The irradiation temperatures assumed in the underlying evaluation is 900 K for the mid-radius MOX agglomerates.
